Designing Subsea Kinematics for RoboSub

Developing subsea hardware requires balancing mechanical strength with watertight isolation. In this post, I detail how we redesigned housing seals to prevent micro-leaks during deepwater operation.

Hydrostatic Compression Seals

Under deep ambient pressures, standard O-ring configurations can deform without uniform compression support. By shifting to custom seal geometry, we maintained consistent sealing limits.

Testing under high-pressure chambers verified zero fluid penetration across extended duty cycles.
